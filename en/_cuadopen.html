Pixeria method

Less demo. More system.

  1. Diagnose

    Understand audience, objective, constraints, legal sensitivity, budget and channels.

  2. Design

    Turn the brief into a repeatable pipeline: models, prompts, assets, roles and control points.

  3. Produce

    Create versions with judgement, review quality, measure cost and document decisions.

  4. Publish

    Prepare deliverables, adapt channels, preserve learnings and feed the next cycle.

Principles that don't expire

  1. Control

    We pick the tool that delivers a reproducible result, not the one trending this week. The model changes; the judgment doesn't.

  2. Rights

    Every asset with a clear license and traceable provenance: no legal surprises downstream.

  3. Judgment

    AI speeds things up; the decision of what ships and what doesn't is always a person's.

  4. Measurement

    We document which model, prompt and cost gave the best result, so we can repeat it tomorrow.
