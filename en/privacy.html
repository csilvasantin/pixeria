Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This page explains what personal data Pixeria processes, for what purpose and legal basis, and how to exercise your rights. Pixeria is an AI content creation project run by its owner; the contact for any privacy matter is hola@admiranext.com.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the radar, we store your email to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or writing to hola@admiranext.com. We do not share your email with third parties for commercial purposes.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. These cookies are not activated until you accept them in the consent banner (Consent Mode v2: analytics is off by default). We also use our own technical storage (localStorage) to remember your cookie decision; this is necessary for the notice to work and does not require consent.

Cookie / storageTypePurpose
pixeria_consent (localStorage)TechnicalRemember whether you accepted or rejected cookies
_ga, _ga_* (Google)Analytics (after acceptance)Aggregate audience measurement

You can change your decision by clearing the site data in your browser, which will show the banner again.

Who processes your data

  • Google (Analytics) — audience measurement, only after your consent.
  • Cloudflare — infrastructure where the subscriber list is securely stored.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

Retention

We keep your email while you remain subscribed or until you request removal. Analytics data is retained in aggregate per the Google Analytics configuration.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to hola@admiranext.com. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

This document is a baseline policy for a personal/professional site. If Pixeria operates as a company or processes data at larger scale, review it with legal advice to adapt it to your case.
