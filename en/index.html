A living guide

Creating with AI is no longer about trying scattered tools.

Pixeria maps the new territory of creation: models, prompts, pipelines, rights, costs, quality, distribution and editorial judgement.

The goal is to help a team move from an idea to a publishable asset without getting lost in demos, hype and tool noise. In production, it powers Pixer Feed: the system that pushes generated image, video and audio to screens and live events.

Pixeria method

Less demo. More system.

  1. Diagnose

    Understand audience, objective, constraints, legal sensitivity, budget and channels.

  2. Design

    Turn the brief into a repeatable pipeline: models, prompts, assets, roles and control points.

  3. Produce

    Create versions with judgement, review quality, measure cost and document decisions.

  4. Publish

    Prepare deliverables, adapt channels, preserve learnings and feed the next cycle.
