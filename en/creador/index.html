        Player scheduling

        Schedules the admira.tv default playlist each screen plays (canal.html?screen=…). First a test run that changes nothing; then the real run, after confirmation. The admira.tv key is added by the server: it is never asked for here.

          Scheduling log

            Choose sizes in the library to create your adaptations.

            Detail first: high-precision resizing and more efficient compression within the same resolution and bitrate limits. Export may take longer; enlarging a small source cannot recover missing detail.

            Output types and limits

            Export MP4 with your settings and the original audio, when present. Every export joins the queue in the bottom-right corner and runs in the background in this browser, one variant at a time: keep working or switch video. Source limit 100 MB; 4K can take several minutes. Large batches must be split to stay within the memory budget. The first export downloads the engine (about 32 MB). Expand uses a blurred background without AI; blur may look slightly different from the preview. Input FPS and bitrate are estimates.

            Social and digital: MP4. Display and print: PNG of the current frame; pause the video to choose it. Print: RGB at 150 ppi, without bleed or CMYK. Sizes are references: check file weight, safe areas and destination requirements. Compatibility affects the four aspect-ratio formats; sized presets keep their resolution.

            Still image: display and print come out as PNG (display also as JPG); social, digital, project formats and video walls as H.264 MP4 at 25 fps with the length you choose (1–60 s, 10 by default), no audio. Still ads recompose complete copy; Advanced offers Recreate with AI to generate missing surroundings. An SVG is rasterised at each format's resolution. An animated GIF becomes an H.264 MP4 at 25 fps lasting one loop; PNG and JPG take the current frame.

            Create: when the format is very different from the content (disproportion r ≥ 3.5, e.g. a 16:9 on a 3840×540 banner), the card switches to «Create» and offers three local recipes: moments strip, pan sweep and moving ticker. If the project ficha sets a recipe for a format, that format always creates, even with a lower r. You can force «Adapt» or «Create» on any card; recipe settings live in Advanced. ▶ Preview animation shows the movement before exporting (also with a still image or a paused video), and the time bar jumps to any instant.

            Project notes

            Segmented video walls: reframing happens on the physical wall and every screen is cut from the same master. The delivery is one MP4 with one cell per screen; you can also export one silent MP4 per screen, with the same duration at 25 fps. The picture only flows across screens if the players start together.
